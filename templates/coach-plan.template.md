# Coaching plan

**This is a starter.** Replace the example thread with your own, or ask `/coach` to seed a plan from your run log, your JTBD doc and your commitments ledger once you have a week of briefs. `coach.py` parses this file and nothing else, so editing it changes the coaching the next morning.

**What the loop does with it.** One nudge a morning, on one thread. Never the same thread two mornings running. A thread you decline twice parks itself for a month.

**The frame, in every nudge:** what is in your control, what is not and can be put down, and one thing you can do today.

---

## `closure` · Turn what you notice into an owner and a date, the same day

- **priority:** 1
- **horizon:** end of this quarter

**Target state.** Anything you raise has a named owner and a date within 24 hours, or it is closed as overtaken. Carrying an item is a decision with a trigger attached, never a default.

**In your control:** whether something you noticed leaves your head with an owner and a date. **Not in your control:** how fast that owner moves.
